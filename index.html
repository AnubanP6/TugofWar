Tug-of-War Showdown

Teacher Jonny Edition

Game settings

Game mode
Team A keys: A S D F or tap the answer on the left.
Team B keys: J K L ; or tap the answer on the right.

Both teams see the same question at the same time. The first team to tap the correct answer pulls the rope. A wrong answer locks that team out of the question, so the other team can still win it.

Your questions

You can paste rows straight from Excel (tab-separated) as well. Add 1 to 3 wrong answers per question. Lines starting with # are ignored.

The questions are stored inside the link itself, so students don't need anything else. If you change your questions, copy a new link.

Race the robot! 🤖

Robot level

Tap the correct answer before the robot does. On a keyboard, press A S D F or 1 2 3 4. A wrong answer locks you out of that question.
